Title: Credit Card Application Scorecard for Credit Risk Assessment

Problem Statement:

Financial institutions receive thousands of credit card applications every month. Approving applications without accurately assessing the applicant's creditworthiness can lead to increased default rates, financial losses, and higher provisioning costs. On the other hand, rejecting creditworthy applicants results in lost business opportunities and reduced customer acquisition.

The objective of this project is to develop a Credit Card Application Scorecard that predicts the probability of a customer defaulting within a specified performance period (e.g., 12 months) based on demographic, financial, employment, and credit bureau information available at the time of application. The scorecard will classify applicants into different risk categories, enabling the bank to make informed lending decisions while maintaining an optimal balance between portfolio growth and credit risk.

The model will use historical customer data to estimate the likelihood of default and assign a risk score to each applicant. This score will assist the credit underwriting team in automating approval decisions, reducing manual intervention, minimizing credit losses, and improving the overall quality of the credit card portfolio.
